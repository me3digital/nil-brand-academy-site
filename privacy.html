How we measure whether an advertisement worked

Our checkout is hosted by Kit, and it does not pass the advertising click identifier through to us. So when a purchase happens we cannot tell on our own which advertisement produced it.

To close that gap we send Google a hashed version of your email address alongside the purchase. Hashing converts your address into a fixed string of characters using SHA-256, a one-way process: it cannot be reversed back into your email. Google compares that string against its own records to work out whether the purchase followed one of our advertisements, and reports the result to us as a count, never as a name or an address.

We never send Google your actual email address, and we do not use this to build a profile of you or to target you with further advertising. If you would rather not participate, you can control personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center, or email us and we will exclude your purchase from this matching.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers for their own independent use.

Cookies and your choices

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We use them to measure site usage and to remember which advertisement, if any, brought you here so that we can attribute a sale correctly.

  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking them will not prevent you from reading this site or buying the kit.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics entirely using Google's browser opt-out add-on.
  • You can control personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center.
  • Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link that takes effect immediately.
