THE PART NOBODY TEACHES YOU

Why some athletes get the call, and others get ignored.

When a brand is deciding whether to work with you, there is a quiet conversation happening in a room you never see. Before anyone talks about money, they are asking a handful of questions about you. Answer them well, and you get the yes. Here is what they are actually asking:

Can we trust this athlete with our name?
Will they represent us the way we would represent ourselves?
Will they respond professionally and on time?
Will they actually hit the deadline we agreed on?
Do they understand this is a business relationship?
Can they communicate clearly when something changes?

Notice what is not on that list: your follower count. None of these questions are about talent or fame. They are about whether you are prepared. And every single one of them is something you can learn to answer with confidence.

No. This whole Kit is built on the opposite idea: brands hire athletes they trust and can work with, not just big accounts. Many first deals go to athletes with small, engaged, local audiences.

Absolutely, and many do. Parents and athletes work through it together all the time. It is written in plain English, with no jargon, so anyone can follow it.

A free Canva account covers the Canva versions of the templates, and the Google Docs versions work without it. Either way, you are covered.

You can read the Guide in an afternoon. The Workbook and templates are meant to be worked through at your pace, and there is a 30-Day Action Plan if you want a simple week-by-week rhythm.

Yes. It is designed for athletes starting from zero. Every step is explained, and the templates give you a professional starting point so you are never staring at a blank page.

Can I update my media kit later?

Yes. The templates are yours to keep and edit as often as you want, as your stats, story, and partnerships grow.

How do I get everything?

Instantly. It is a digital download, so you get access right after purchase. Nothing ships. Works on any device.

No, it is educational. We flag what to watch, like FTC disclosure and school and state rules, and the Resources Hub links you to the official sources so you can verify your own current rules.
